## How this was run

- 53 real sites (`sites.json`), one purpose each, expectations written before any run.
- Two network runs per site with the skill's own polite client (`run-real-sites.mjs record`), then the clauses of the terms classified by the model (`classifications.json`, with the judgement calls listed), then a replay of the recorded responses with the classification (`run-real-sites.mjs replay`). About 25 requests per site in all, over three recording passes.
- The table is the final state, after the fixes below.

A one-off personal reading of one page; copying more, or reusing the text, would not be green |

**Expected red, got orange (3): the skill could not read the decisive signal and stayed orange, which is the safe side.**

- stackoverflow (ai-tdm): robots.txt answers HTTP 418 and the page 403: the AI-crawler rules (R3) cannot be read. Now reported as an access restriction (O2).
- ebay (monitor): the page answers 403; the terms link could not be found. Orange O2.
- youtube (monitor): the terms page (`/t/terms`) is disallowed to the skill by robots.txt, so it is not read. Orange O6.

**Expected orange, got red (2): the terms forbid scraping; expectations written without reading them.**

## Defects found on real sites, and fixed

| Defect | Site where it showed | Fix | Test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Challenge pages served with HTTP 200 were taken for real pages (and made runs unstable) | annuaire-entreprises (Imperva), cdiscount (Baleen) | Vendor markers (Imperva, DataDome, PerimeterX, AWS WAF) and near-empty pages mentioning a challenge → O2 | fixture 19 |
| Latin-1 pages decoded as UTF-8: accented keywords did not match | societe.com | Charset from Content-Type or `<meta charset>` | fixture 20, test-keywords |
| Terms links matched on loose words: a news article, "451 Unavailable For Legal Reasons", "DC Terms", "Glossary of Terms", "legal form" | guardian, mdn, hal, legislation.gov.uk, wikidata | Generic words must be the whole link text or a whole path segment; compound labels ("Mentions légales et crédits") use their first part | test-keywords |
| A long list item without full stops collapsed to an empty quote "…": the key scraping clause was lost | wttj, github | Cut around the keyword instead | test-keywords |
| "automatique / automatically" picked payment and update clauses (12 noise clauses on one site) | lemonde, gov.uk, amazon | Keyword limited to "automated / automatisé / moyens automatiques" | test-keywords |
| Rules on scraping in a page linked from the terms were missed | github (Acceptable Use Policies) | Follow strong links (acceptable use, terms) found in terms pages, within the 3-page budget, acceptable-use first | real-site re-run |
| robots.txt answering 401 / 403 / 418 / 429 counted as "no rules" | stackoverflow, fnac, npm | Reported as an access restriction (O2) | decision table |
| Stability metric counted requests | stackoverflow | Compares verdict, codes and clause ids only | — |

## Open points

- **O3 decided (2026-10-04):** a TDM reservation or an AI-crawler block now makes only `bulk` orange; for `lookup`, `monitor` and `republish` it is a note. hal (lookup) became green (G4); lemonde-lookup became green once the classification rules were made explicit (a generic copyright notice is unrelated to a lookup).
- **Keyword noise** remains ("Les meilleurs robots aspirateurs laveurs", "This field is for robots only", definitions of "Réutilisation"): harmless, since the model labels them `unrelated`, but it costs reading time.
- **Terms that cannot be found** on many sites (JavaScript footers, terms on another domain such as wikimedia.org or osmfoundation.org): orange O6, the safe side, but a recall limit.
- **Single classifier.** All clauses were labelled by one model in one session; a second, independent labelling (or a headless run of the full skill) would show how much the verdicts depend on the classifier.
