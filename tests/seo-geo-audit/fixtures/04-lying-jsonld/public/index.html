One grinder, done properly

Brewline is a two-person company in Portland that makes a single product: the C1 hand grinder. We designed it because entry-level electric grinders produce too many fines for a clean pour-over, and good hand grinders cost more than most people want to spend on their first serious setup.

The C1 uses a 38 mm stainless-steel conical burr, a dual-bearing shaft so the burr does not wobble, and 30 click-stops per rotation. It grinds 20 grams for a pour-over in about 40 seconds. Every grinder is assembled and calibrated by hand before shipping.
