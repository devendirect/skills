Moulin de Kerhuel holiday cottage

An 18th-century watermill restored as a holiday cottage for six, beside a stream in Plouhinec, in the Morbihan area of southern Brittany. The beaches of the Ria d'Étel are four kilometres away, and the Carnac standing stones twenty minutes by car.

The cottage has three bedrooms, a large living room with a fireplace, a fully equipped kitchen and an 800 m² enclosed garden with a terrace. Bed linen and towels are provided, and end-of-stay cleaning is included.

Weekly rentals, Saturday to Saturday, from April to October. Prices: €750 per week in April, May, June, September and October; €1,150 in July and August.
