About Fieldnotes

Fieldnotes is written by a single amateur birdwatcher based in Saint-Brevin-les-Pins, who has walked the Loire estuary every week since 2019. It is not affiliated with any association and carries no advertising.

Every sighting is counted on site with binoculars and a telescope, at the time and place stated in the note. Counts are rounded for large flocks and flagged as estimates. Rare species are only published after the observation has been confirmed, and exact locations of sensitive nesting sites are never given.

Notes are published every Monday. Corrections are made in place, with the date of the correction at the bottom of the note. If you spot an error, the contact address is on the home page.
