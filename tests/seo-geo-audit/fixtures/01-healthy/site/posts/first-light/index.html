First light at Paimbœuf: avocets and the first brent geese

28 September 2026 · High tide 08:42 (coefficient 94) · Paimbœuf dyke, east of the harbour.

The rising tide pushed the flocks against the dyke from 07:40. The count at 08:30, one hour before high water, gave 340 avocets in one tight group, 12 spoonbills resting on the eastern sandbank, around 600 dunlins and the first 25 brent geese of the season, arriving from the west in two small skeins.

The brent geese are early this year: the first birds usually appear in the second week of October. A single osprey fished the channel between 08:10 and 08:25 before heading south.

Access: park at the harbour, walk 800 metres east along the dyke. Stay on the path, the sandbanks are a protected resting area at high tide.
